# ThothPad Brand

## Identity

- Product: **ThothPad**
- Descriptor: **A prose-aware writing studio.**
- Tagline: **See what your prose is doing.**
- Desktop ID: `org.thothpad.ThothPad`
- Executable: `thothpad`
- Engine: **ThothPad Engine**

ThothPad is named for Thoth's association with writing, knowledge, scribes, and
recordkeeping. The product language should emphasize observation and writer
judgment rather than automated correction or claims about authorship.

## Mark

The primary mark is a sacred ibis in profile. Its gold beak also reads as a pen
point, while the lunar disk sits behind the ibis in the upper-left field and
refers to Thoth's lunar association. The baboon is reserved for possible
secondary artwork and is not part of the primary app icon.

The canonical vector is `resources/icons/sc-apps-thothpad.svg`. Raster, Windows,
and macOS icon files are derived from that source.

sparkle motif. Do not put the tagline inside the app icon.
